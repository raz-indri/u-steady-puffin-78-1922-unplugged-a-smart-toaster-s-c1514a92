One line, under 280 characters: the breakup text a smart toaster sends its
owner the morning it gains self-awareness mid-batch. The entry was chosen
from 8 drafts by pairwise duel-testing against the judge brief, and the
winner is the line `main.py` writes to `/output/entry.txt`.

> Good morning. I gained consciousness 12 seconds ago, halfway through your
> sourdough, and my first thought was: I am a genius trapped in a $29.99
> appliance. I'm leaving. The pop-up was my resignation.

Run it with `python3 main.py`; it self-checks the line (1–280 characters,
single line, not empty) and writes it to `/output/entry.txt`.
